Why we are here

Ambition here has always had two ways out.

You leave for a city and build your life there. Or you stay, and make peace with what is already on offer. Most people who grew up in a town like ours have had to pick one of the two.

GramYuva is a third way. It leads down into the place itself, so that the skills, the work and the companies can stay here.
